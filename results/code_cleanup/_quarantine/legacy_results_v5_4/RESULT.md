# V5.4 Result

## Scope

V5.4 added capability-driven screening plus engineering actuator/sensor fidelity. It attempted to infer aircraft control authority with a fixed 0.6 s fractional pulse.

## Validation finding

V5.4.1 demonstrated that the 0.6 s method selected against fast responses: a fast aircraft can cross the ±45° operational Roll bound before the fixed sample time and be rejected by the old safety gate. Therefore the V5.4 maximum-rate and derived Level conclusions were invalidated.

## Superseded conclusions

- Do not use the V5.4 fractional-pulse authority numbers for Level selection.
- Do not treat V5.4 candidate files as current recommendations without V5.4.1 completion/repeatability.
- V5.4 engineering sensor/actuator support remains useful infrastructure, but most non-left-aileron hardware values are not measured UAV_A calibration.

## Final conclusion

V5.4 is historical method-development evidence. The authoritative capability and completion result is `../v5_4_1/RESULT.md`.
